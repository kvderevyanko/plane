# LR1600 Ground Operations Architecture v3

This supersedes the earlier 2,400-g ground screen. It is a preliminary 2,600-g integration design for dirt, compact soil and grass: wheels in summer and axle-mounted skis in winter. It does not alter wing geometry or claim certification.

Selected serviceable configuration: individually replaceable GFRP main spring legs, 105-mm mains and a 75-mm nose wheel on a replaceable sprung/compliant strut and fork. The nose wheel is fixed on aircraft +X by a positive anti-rotation mechanical index, with no steering linkage or yaw degree of freedom. Contacts are main X=+125, Y=+/-175 and nose X=-250: wheelbase=375 mm, track=350 mm, static nose share=13--17% over the design CG band. The 3-mm birch main box uses frames +65/+130/+200; the 3-mm nose keel box uses -285/-170/-55. Carbon longerons bridge them. Foam, outer skin and printed fairings have no primary-load credit.

The 13x10 pusher at X=+430, Z=+50 retains **66.9-mm** full-rough tip clearance with 105-mm wheels: 18-mm compression, 8-deg tail-low, one-main roll sensitivity, 20-mm rut and 5-mm wear/build are included. Corrected 100-mm wheels give 64.4 mm: above the 60-mm minimum but below the preferred 65-mm margin. The 14-in prop is a secondary case at approximately 54.2 mm and is not a rough-field baseline.

The governing main hardpoint proof is 121 N vertical at one wheel; nose proof is 60 N vertical and both sites receive 35 N lateral/longitudinal proof. Test complete bays, not a plywood coupon. The wheel configuration is a 196-g estimate; winter skis/retained legs are a 232-g estimate after deleting the former 6-g steering-only allowance. Main and nose ski pivots reuse exactly the wheel axles/hardpoints, with metal bushes, tip-up straps and positive nose-down stops. The nose ski may pitch about its axle but remains yaw-locked by the strut-root index. See [landing-gear.md](landing-gear.md) for calculations, failure modes and release gates.

Lower nose/pack/antenna protection is a replaceable skid/bumper, recessed FPV window and grommeted strain-relieved penetrations. Replace a bent leg, axle or damaged skid rather than accepting permanent set or bond damage after a rough impact.
